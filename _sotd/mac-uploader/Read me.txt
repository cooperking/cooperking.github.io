SOTD Uploader
=============

Puts each new or changed MP3 and MP4 in your SOTD folder on the Song of the Day site by itself, as the admin page's
folder upload does. Each video then gets its TV copy made, as usual.

Setting up
1. Double-click "Install.command". If macOS says it can't be opened (it isn't from the App Store), open
   System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message about Install.command, click Open Anyway, and
   double-click it again.
2. Choose your SOTD folder, then type the admin page password.
That's it: it runs in the background, when the folder changes and every 15 minutes, and starts again with the Mac.

What it sends
- Files whose names clearly match one song: "Artist - Title", "Title Artist" or just the title.
- Only new or changed files: anything already on the site is recognised and left.
- A file still being exported or copied waits until it has stopped changing for a minute.
- A file it can't match is left alone; if it still can't place it after 6 hours (today's song may not be on the
  playlist yet), you get a notification, and can rename it or upload it on the admin page.

Notifications tell you what went up. The full record is in
~/Library/Application Support/SOTD Uploader/uploader.log

If nothing goes up and the folder is in Documents, Desktop or Downloads, macOS may be stopping it from reading
there: System Settings > Privacy & Security > Full Disk Access, click +, press Cmd+Shift+G, type
/usr/bin/python3, and add it. (Or keep the SOTD folder somewhere else, such as your home folder.)

To change the folder or the password, double-click Install.command again. To stop it, double-click Uninstall.command.
