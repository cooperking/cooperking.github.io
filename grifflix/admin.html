        Looking…

        Grifflix keeps its own copy of the films, TV, collections and Movie of the Day, so it shows them with the Mac mini asleep: the Mac is only asked to play. After changing films on the Mac, Update puts them on the site.

          Movie of the Day

          Each one a surprise

          Each day’s film shows on the calendar at 8am that day, when the alert goes out, for everyone, you too. Switch this on to see what’s coming (only you).

          Monthly themes

          Each theme’s film on its 1st

          The film behind each month’s TV theme as Movie of the Day on the 1st of its month (the theme named in its note). The films Grifflix hasn’t got yet are left for later. It changes Movie of the Day for everyone once you next press Update everything.

            Downloads

            Looking…

            Members can keep a film on their phone or computer (a Download button on each film; not the Fire TV app). It comes from the Mac, so the Mac must be on while it downloads.

              Fonts

              Futura

              Your Futura web fonts (.woff2 and .woff), kept in Grifflix’s own storage, not on GitHub: pick them all at once (Book, Bold, Light).

              The monthly themes’ own fonts, when you have them, named like this (each takes over from the stand-in at once): helvetica-bold (January, The Shining’s credits), eurostile-extended (March, Cherbourg), clarendon-bold (April, Vertigo’s titles), ocr-a (May, The Matrix), wood-gothic (July, All the President’s Men’s poster), each .woff2 or .woff.
