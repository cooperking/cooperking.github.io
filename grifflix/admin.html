Updates

Looking…

    One button: the Mac’s Grifflix files brought up to date (if there’s an update), the films brought over from the Mac, and, if the draft has changes, the draft put live for everyone (listed above). Look at the Preview first.

    Preview
    Members

    Members

    Invite someone, or send a member a new link. Each invitation has its own card for Messages and WhatsApp, and works on up to 5 devices for 30 days.

      Households

      Who lives together

      Everyone in a household can send films to, and work, any Fire TV signed in by one of them (Play on the TV). Members not in a household see only their own TVs. Members can also make their own, with a code from their profile; they show here too.

        Update from the Mac

        Looking…

        Grifflix keeps its own copy of the films, TV, collections and Movie of the Day, so it shows them with the Mac mini asleep: the Mac is only asked to play. After changing films on the Mac, Update puts them on the site.

        Conversions

        Looking…

          Playing now

          What’s playing

          Who’s watching what, on which device, and whether the Mac mini is converting it as it plays (slow work for the old Mac) or sending it as it is.

            Subtitles

            Subtitles, in time

            Members find subtitles for a film in the player (the subtitles button, then Find subtitles…). They come from OpenSubtitles, and the Mac mini times each one to the film’s sound. It needs your OpenSubtitles account and an API key, kept on the Mac only: make a free account at opensubtitles.com, then get a key at opensubtitles.com/en/consumers (“New consumer”, any name).

            Library

            Every film’s quality

            Each film’s file as it is on the Mac: its picture, format, rate, size and sound, graded Good, OK or Replace (standard definition, or too little picture for its size), with those converted and those waiting to be.

              Movie of the Day

              Each one a surprise

              Each day’s film shows on the calendar at 8am that day, when the alert goes out, for everyone, you too. Switch this on to see what’s coming (only you).

              A day’s film

              Change a day

              Choose a day to change its film or take it off (only here now, not on the site). A day still to come stays a surprise: you change it without seeing what it was. Changes are made on the Mac; Update everything puts them on the site.

              Looking…
                Themes

                The months and their themes

                Each year’s twelve months in their themes. Drag any theme from The themes below onto a month (on a phone, hold then drag), or a month onto another to swap them; tap one to see it working. Save keeps it and shows it on the draft; Update everything puts it live for everyone.

                Looking at the films…

                The themes

                Drag one onto any month above (a theme can be on as many months as you like). Drag a month here, or tap its ×, to put it back as it was.

                Open it full screen

                Saving puts each month’s theme film on its 1st: Grifflix’s own copy, or its trailer if Grifflix hasn’t got it (that film goes on your Requests). Update everything puts them on the site.

                Downloads

                Looking…

                Members can keep a film on their phone or computer (a Download button on each film; not the Fire TV app). It comes from the Mac, so the Mac must be on while it downloads.

                Films to get

                Films Grifflix hasn’t got yet: a month’s theme film is added here when you save it on a month (its trailer stands in on the 1st till then), and you can add your own. Each one says when it’s in Grifflix.

                  Fonts

                  Futura

                  Your own fonts (.woff2, .woff, .ttf or .otf, or a .zip of them), kept in Grifflix’s own storage, not on GitHub: Futura (Book, Bold, Light), and the themes’ own, such as Magneto for The Lane (name it magneto).

                  The monthly themes’ own fonts, when you have them, named like this (each takes over from the stand-in at once): helvetica-bold (The Shining’s credits), eurostile-extended (Cherbourg), clarendon-bold (Vertigo’s titles), ocr-a (The Matrix), wood-gothic (All the President’s Men’s poster), each .woff2 or .woff.
