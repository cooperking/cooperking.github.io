                          Subtitles made ready
                          
        
                          English subtitles, timed by what’s said
                          
        
                          How it works
        
                          For each foreign-language film with no English subtitles, tried in this order, stopping at the first that works:
                          
        
                            
          
                          1. English subtitles made for this very copy (OpenSubtitles knows the file by its fingerprint): used as they are. Added.
                            2. 
          
                          2. English named for this very release (uploaded under the name your copy was downloaded as, or its source and group) and of the film’s length: used as it is. Added.
                            3. 
          
                          3. A subtitle in another language made for this copy (by its fingerprint, or uploaded under the name the copy was downloaded as): an English subtitle timed to its lines, on the Mac mini. Corrected.
                            4. 
          
                          4. If its timing alone doesn’t line it up: that subtitle translated on your Mac and compared line by line with the English ones. Corrected.
                            5. 
          
                          5. Whisper on your Mac, only if nothing else fits: four parts of the film listened to, and an English subtitle timed to what’s said. Corrected, or To check if the timing couldn’t be confirmed.
                            6. 
          
                          6. If nothing fits: Not found, with why, and the closest to choose yourself.
                            7. 
        
                          
        
                          Open any film for what was done, the versions there are, and its dialogue side by side: each line said, the English line that showed there before, and the one that shows now. Choose another version to see it, and use it if it's better.

                                Matching
                                
        
                                Films matched by TMDB
                                
        
                                With your TMDB key on the Mac mini, a film the media server has taken for another (No shown as No Country for Old Men) is put right by itself, by TMDB’s own details, its length included, so two films of the same name (the two Good Boys) are told apart. Get a key at themoviedb.org/settings/api (the “API Key”, or the “API Read Access Token”); your old get_metadata.py script has one in it too. Kept on the Mac only.
                                
        
                                
      
                                
      
                                
        
                                Subtitles
                                
        
                                Subtitles, in time
                                
        
                                Members find subtitles for a film in the player (the subtitles button, then Find subtitles…). They come from OpenSubtitles, and the Mac mini times each one to the film’s sound. It needs your OpenSubtitles account and an API key, kept on the Mac only: make a free account at opensubtitles.com, then get a key at opensubtitles.com/en/consumers (“New consumer”, any name).
