          Movie of the Day

          Each one a surprise

          Each day’s film shows on the calendar at 8am that day, when the alert goes out, for everyone, you too. Switch this on to see what’s coming (only you).

          Monthly themes

          The months and their themes

          The themes in order, as many as you like: January 2026 has the first, each month the next, and round again at the end (twelve: each month its own). Choose, move with the arrows, add from Not used now, or take one out with ×. Save changes the site for everyone at once (no Update needed). Under each: whether its film is in Grifflix, and whether it’s Movie of the Day on that month’s next 1st. Try opens the draft in that theme.

            Not used now

              Putting the films on their 1sts changes Movie of the Day for everyone once you next press Update everything. Films Grifflix hasn’t got are left until it has.

                Fonts

                Futura

                Your Futura web fonts (.woff2 and .woff), kept in Grifflix’s own storage, not on GitHub: pick them all at once (Book, Bold, Light).

                The monthly themes’ own fonts, when you have them, named like this (each takes over from the stand-in at once): helvetica-bold (The Shining’s credits), eurostile-extended (Cherbourg), clarendon-bold (Vertigo’s titles), ocr-a (The Matrix), wood-gothic (All the President’s Men’s poster), each .woff2 or .woff.
