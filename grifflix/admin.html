Duplicates

Films you have twice

For each film, the copy marked is kept and the others are set aside, into “Set aside/Duplicates” on the G-Drive (not deleted: empty it when you’re happy). The best copy is chosen for you (the sharpest picture, then the most detail); choose another if you’d rather. Calendars, lists and hearts follow the copy kept, and its extras stay with it.

            Once, on your Mac, in Terminal:

                Matching
                
        
                Films matched by TMDB
                
        
                With your TMDB key on the Mac mini, a film the media server has taken for another (No shown as No Country for Old Men) is put right by itself, by TMDB’s own details, its length included, so two films of the same name (the two Good Boys) are told apart. Get a key at themoviedb.org/settings/api (the “API Key”, or the “API Read Access Token”); your old get_metadata.py script has one in it too. Kept on the Mac only.
                
        
                
      
                
      
                
        
                Subtitles
                
        
                Subtitles, in time
                
        
                Members find subtitles for a film in the player (the subtitles button, then Find subtitles…). They come from OpenSubtitles, and the Mac mini times each one to the film’s sound. It needs your OpenSubtitles account and an API key, kept on the Mac only: make a free account at opensubtitles.com, then get a key at opensubtitles.com/en/consumers (“New consumer”, any name).
                
        
                
          
          
          
        
                
        
                
      
                
      
                
        
                Library
                
        
                Every film’s quality
                
        
                Each film’s file as it is on the Mac: its picture, format, rate, size and sound, by its size (4K, 1080p, 720p, SD) and judged against that size: Good; Replace, a poor copy for its size (too little picture for its rate); or Convert, bigger than 1080p or a bigger file than its size needs. All of it can be copied into a Google Sheet.

                      Criterion
                      
        
                      Criterion’s covers
                      
        
                      Every Criterion cover, as Criterion makes it, on each Criterion film in Grifflix (and in your Google Drive’s “Criterion images”, and new films added to the Criterion sheet). Criterion’s site only lets a person’s own browser see its list, so: open Criterion’s list, let it load, then File → Save Page As… (Web Page, HTML Only, or in Safari Page Source; not “Complete”), and choose that file here. Only when Criterion has added films to its list since you last did: every Criterion film you bring in is checked against the list you last gave, by itself, and gets its cover, spine number and place in The Criterion Collection.

                      Fonts
                      
        
                      Futura
                      
        
                      Your own fonts (.woff2, .woff, .ttf or .otf, or a .zip of them), kept in Grifflix’s own storage, not on GitHub: Futura (Book, Bold, Light), and the themes’ own, such as Magneto for The Lane (name it magneto).
