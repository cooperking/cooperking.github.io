Why am I seeing this ad?

The videos here play in YouTube’s own player. YouTube chooses when to show an advert and keeps what it earns from it. This site earns nothing from the adverts and can’t turn them off.

Without YouTube Premium

Many adverts have a Skip button after 5 seconds. Or press Close video to hear the song’s 30-second preview instead: previews have no adverts.

Premium: Chrome or Edge

Sign in to YouTube in the same browser. Its cookies are allowed here unless you’ve changed that. If adverts still show, in Chrome go to Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies and add [*.]youtube.com under “Allowed to use third-party cookies”.

↑ Index

Premium: iPhone or iPad

Sign in to YouTube in Safari. Then go to Settings → Apps → Safari (on iOS 17 and earlier, Settings → Safari) and turn off Prevent Cross-Site Tracking. This applies to every website, not only this one. If you’d rather not, watch on YouTube.

↑ Index

Premium: Safari on a Mac

Sign in to YouTube in Safari. Then go to Safari → Settings → Privacy and untick Prevent cross-site tracking. This applies to every website, not only this one.

↑ Index

Premium: Firefox

Sign in to YouTube in Firefox. Then, on this site, click the shield to the left of the address bar and turn off Enhanced Tracking Protection for it.

↑ Index

Premium: any browser

Press the YouTube logo in the player to watch the song on YouTube itself, or On YouTube at the top of the calendar for the whole playlist. Premium works there, and on a phone it opens the YouTube app.

The Fire TV app

The app can’t sign in to YouTube, so adverts show even with Premium. Close the video to hear the preview instead, or play the playlist in the YouTube app.

Why Premium isn’t always recognised here

Premium only removes adverts when YouTube’s player knows it’s you. In a video on another website, it can only tell if you’re signed in to YouTube in the same browser and the browser lets YouTube use its cookies on that website. YouTube’s help says:

“If you’re watching a YouTube video embedded on a website, make sure that you aren’t blocking YouTube cookies.”
