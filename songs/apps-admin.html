Where to sign in and look after each app, and the services behind them. All three apps open the live calendar, so a change to the calendar reaches them with no new build; an app needs a new build only when its own part changes.

iPhone

iPhone app

On TestFlight. Each build lasts 90 days; the job makes a new one on the 1st of every second month.

    • App Store Connectyour Apple IDThe app → TestFlight: its builds, the family’s group (SOTD) and its public link, Beta App Review.
    • App Store Connect: API keysyour Apple IDThe key the jobs upload builds with (the secrets ASC_KEY_ID, ASC_ISSUER_ID, ASC_KEY_P8).
    • Apple Developer: Certificates, Identifiers & Profilesyour Apple IDThe app’s identifier (Associated Domains, push notifications), and under Keys the push key (APNS_KEY_ID, APNS_KEY_P8).
    • GitHub: the “iPhone app” jobBuilds the app on GitHub’s Mac and sends it to TestFlight. Run it by hand with Run workflow.
    • GitHub: the “iPhone app, public link” jobPuts the newest build to Beta App Review and the public link in songs/ios.json, where invitations find it. Turns itself off when done: Enable workflow to have it look again after a new build.
    Android

    Android app

    On Google Play as a closed test, and on the website as an app to install directly.

      Google Play’s testers

      The closed test’s testers come from its email list in Play Console, Song of the Day testers.

      1. Download the emails (below): every member with an email address, one a line. Only a Google account’s address works on Google Play.
      2. Open the test’s testers in Play Console (below). Under Email lists, the arrow by Song of the Day testers opens the list: Upload CSV file, then Save changes. (Or Copy the emails and paste them into Add email addresses.)
      3. Email lists chosen (not Google Groups), the list ticked, then Save. Play says the change goes to Publishing overview, to send for review there.
      4. They join the test with the opt-in link, which their invitation gives them on Android.
      Open the test’s testers in Play Console
      • Play Consoleyour own Google account, then the developer account John Griffiths (not Collabco’s)Test and release › Testing › Closed testing: the releases and the testers (the button above goes straight to the testers). For production, Google first wants 12 testers opted in for 14 days in a row.
      • Join the test (the link members open)Works once their email is on the test’s email list. Invitations give it on Android to members with an email address.
      • The app on Google PlayShows only for testers who have joined.
      • GitHub: the “Android app” jobBuilds the website’s app (songs/android.apk) and the bundle for Google Play: the run’s artifact sotd-android is the .aab to upload in Play Console as a new closed-testing release.
      • GitHub: the “Android app check” jobRuns the app on an emulated phone and saves what it saw.
      Fire TV

      Fire TV app

      Not in Amazon’s app store: installed with the Downloader app from cooperking.github.io/songs/tv.apk. On opening, it offers each newer build itself.

        • How to install it (the page for members)Downloader, the setting that lets it install apps, then signing in with a code.
        • GitHub: the “Fire TV app” jobBuilds the app and puts it on the website (songs/tv.apk, songs/tv.json).
        • GitHub: the “Fire TV app check” jobRuns the app in an Android TV emulator and works it with the remote’s keys.
        • Alexa developer consolethe Amazon account the Echo usesThe Alexa skill, if it’s set up (the steps: _sotd/alexa/README.md). Its beta testers last 90 days.
        Behind them

        The services

        • Cloudflare: Workers & Pagesyour Cloudflare accountsotd-playlists: the Worker behind members, sign-in, notifications and the song list. Its logs, settings and secrets; its KV store keeps the members.
        • Cloudflare: R2your Cloudflare accountYour recordings and videos for members.
        • GitHub: the sitecooperkingThe calendar (GitHub Pages) and every job.
        • GitHub: ActionsThe jobs and their runs (the Status tab lists the ones that matter).
        • GitHub: SecretsThe keys the jobs use. The Worker job copies its own onto the Worker on each deploy.
        • Google Cloud consoleyour Google accountAPIs & Services › Credentials: the sign-in for YouTube and Gmail (YT_CLIENT_ID, YT_CLIENT_SECRET), and its consent screen.
        • Apps Scriptyour Google accountSong of the Day mailer: sends the sign-in codes, up to 100 emails a day. After a change, in its folder: clasp push -f, then clasp redeploy with its deployment id.
        • YouTube Studioyour Google accountThe channel the songs’ playlist is on. The playlist gets each song as soon as its video is found.
        • Spotify for Developersyour Spotify accountThe app the Worker reads the playlist with (from 06:00 until the day’s song is in).
        • The Spotify playlistWhere each new song starts.
        • Last.fm: API accountsyour Last.fm accountThe key for the songs’ tags (LASTFM_API_KEY).
        • Claude Codeyour Claude accountRoutines › the song facts task, which finds each new song’s facts.
