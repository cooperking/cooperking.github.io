Why am I seeing this ad?

The videos here play in YouTube’s own player. YouTube decides when to show an advert before or during a video, and keeps what it earns from it. This site earns nothing from the adverts and can’t turn them off.

Without YouTube Premium

  • Many adverts have a Skip button after 5 seconds.
  • Close video to hear the song’s 30-second preview instead. Previews have no adverts.

With YouTube Premium

Premium only removes adverts when YouTube’s player knows it’s you. In a video on another website, it can only tell if you’re signed in to YouTube in the same browser and the browser lets YouTube use its cookies on this site. YouTube’s help says:

“If you’re watching a YouTube video embedded on a website, make sure that you aren’t blocking YouTube cookies.”
  • Chrome or Edge (computer or Android): YouTube’s cookies are allowed unless you’ve changed it. If adverts still show, in Chrome go to Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies and add [*.]youtube.com under “Allowed to use third-party cookies”.
  • Safari on iPhone or iPad: Settings → Apps → Safari (on iOS 17 and earlier, Settings → Safari), then turn off Prevent Cross-Site Tracking. This applies to every website, not only this one.
  • Safari on a Mac: Safari → Settings → Privacy, then untick Prevent cross-site tracking. This also applies to every website.
  • Firefox: on this site, click the shield to the left of the address bar and turn off Enhanced Tracking Protection for it.

Or, in any browser, press the YouTube logo in the player to watch the song on YouTube itself, or On YouTube at the top for the whole playlist. Premium works there, and on a phone it opens the YouTube app.

In the Fire TV app

The app can’t sign in to YouTube, so adverts show even with Premium. Close the video to hear the preview instead, or play the playlist in the YouTube app.
