Family

Add someone and send them the link it gives. Opening it signs that phone, computer or TV in (up to 5 devices, within 30 days); after that they hear the whole song and see your video where you've added one. Remove signs all their devices out. New link gives a fresh one (the old one stops working).

    Songs, videos and sharing pictures

    Your own recording of each song (MP3) and video (MP4) plays for members instead of the preview and YouTube; anyone else, and any day without one, gets the preview and YouTube as before. Picture sets your own picture for the day's sharing link (the album cover until you do; the link's card is remade within a few minutes). To add many at once, choose the folder they're in: each file is matched to its day by its name (best as Artist - Title.mp3); check the matches, then upload.
