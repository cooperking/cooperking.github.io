Members you tick hear your own recordings (MP3s) and see your own videos (MP4s), added on the Songs tab: Can see on each card, neither for someone new until you tick it. Every member can save their playlists. Invite someone and send them the invitation: opening it signs that phone or computer in (up to 5 devices, within 30 days). For a Fire TV or an iPhone Home Screen icon, use Code: six letters to type in once, within 30 minutes (on the TV: ≡ → Sign in; on the Home Screen icon: Sign in at the top). With their email address they can also sign in with a code sent to it from your Gmail, should a passkey fail; for Android, the closed test’s email list takes the members’ emails (Download the emails, below). Under each member: which app each of their devices uses, as it last said.
