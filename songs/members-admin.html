Members hear your own recording of each song and see your own videos (added on the Songs tab), and can save their playlists. Invite someone and send them the invitation: opening it signs that phone or computer in (up to 5 devices, within 30 days). For a Fire TV or an iPhone Home Screen icon, use Code: six letters to type in once, within 30 minutes (on the TV: ≡ → Sign in; on the Home Screen icon: Sign in at the top). With their email address they can also sign in with a code sent to it from your Gmail, should a passkey fail; on Android it’s the Google account for Google Play’s test list.
