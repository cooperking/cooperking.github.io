Privacy

John’s Song of the Day · last updated 21 October 2026

The calendar

The calendar at cooperking.github.io/songs has no accounts, sign-ins, adverts or tracking. The songs you add to “My playlist”, and the name you give it, are saved only in your own browser.

Other services the page uses

Song previews play from Spotify. A song’s video plays in YouTube’s own player, from youtube-nocookie.com, and only after you press Video. Fonts come from Google Fonts. Opening a list “in Spotify” sends its songs’ Spotify IDs, and its name, to the site’s small playlist service, which makes the playlist on Spotify. Each of these services handles what it serves under its own privacy policy.

The Song of the Day Google app

The “Song of the Day” app on Google is used only by John’s own automation. It searches YouTube for each day’s song and keeps the Song of the Day playlist on YouTube up to date. It acts only for the YouTube channel John signed in with, and reads or keeps no one else’s data.
