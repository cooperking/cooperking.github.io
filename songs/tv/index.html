Song of the Day on your Fire TV

The calendar has its own app for an Amazon Fire TV or Fire TV Stick: full screen, worked by the remote, with the videos as big as the TV. It isn’t in Amazon’s app store, so it goes on with a free app called Downloader. It takes about five minutes, once.

1 · Get Downloader

  1. On the Fire TV’s home screen, go to Find (the magnifying glass) and search for Downloader.
  2. Choose Downloader (the orange icon, by AFTVnews) and press Get or Download.

2 · Let Downloader install apps

  1. Go to Settings (the cog, far right) → My Fire TV → Developer options.
  2. Choose Install unknown apps, then Downloader, so it says On.

No Developer options? In My Fire TV, choose About, then press OK on the first line (your Fire TV’s name) seven times, until it says you’re a developer. Go back and it’s there.

3 · Install Song of the Day

  1. Open Downloader. If it asks to get to your files, choose Allow.
  2. In the box on its Home page, type this and press Go:
    cooperking.github.io/songs/tv.apk
  3. When it has downloaded, press Install, then Open. (Downloader may offer to delete the file it downloaded: say Delete, it isn’t needed any more.)

Afterwards it’s in your apps (the button with three squares on the home screen). To keep it on the home row, highlight it, press ☰ on the remote and choose Move to front.

4 · Sign in

To hear John’s own recordings and see his videos on the TV, sign it in with a code:

  1. On your phone, open the calendar, tap your person icon at the top, and choose Get a code.
  2. On the TV, in Song of the Day, press ☰ on the remote and choose Sign in with a code. Type the six letters (they work once, within 30 minutes).

The same ☰ menu has Sign in to YouTube: with YouTube Premium, the videos play with no adverts.

The remote

OK opens a day or presses a button; held down on a day, it adds the song to My playlist (or takes it off). Back closes what’s open. ⏯ plays or pauses. ⏪ ⏩ go to the day (or month) before or after.

Updates

The calendar inside the app is always the latest. When there’s a new version of the app itself, it offers it as it opens: choose Install. The first time, the TV asks to let Song of the Day install apps: turn it on, as for Downloader.
